Frequently Asked

Your questions, answered.

How is my car's value calculated?

CarValue uses your vehicle's original price, age, mileage, vehicle type, and condition to calculate an independent estimate. When available, MarketCheck market data is shown alongside the estimate for comparison.

Can I use this value when selling my car?

CarValue can help you understand possible trade-in, private-party, and dealer retail price ranges. The estimates should be used as a reference when researching or negotiating the sale of your vehicle.

Where does the market value come from?

When available, current market-value information is retrieved from MarketCheck using your vehicle information, VIN, mileage, and ZIP code.

What information do I need?

You'll need your vehicle's make, model, original price, age, mileage, vehicle type, condition, VIN, and ZIP code.

Does vehicle condition affect my estimate?

Yes. Vehicle condition is one of the factors used by CarValue's estimation model, along with age, mileage, original price, and vehicle type.

Can I compare multiple vehicles?

Yes. You can run multiple estimates, and your previous valuations are available from the Past Valuations page while you're signed in.
